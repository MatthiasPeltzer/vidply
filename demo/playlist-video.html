VidPly - Video Playlist

Mixed Media Playlist Audio Playlist HLS Streaming

Features Demonstrated

  • Previous/Next buttons - Navigate between videos seamlessly
  • Auto-advance - Automatically plays the next video when one ends
  • Episode info display - Shows current episode number, title, series, and duration
  • Duration badges - YouTube-style duration overlays on thumbnails
  • Video descriptions - Episode synopses and descriptions (WCAG accessible)
  • Interactive playlist panel - Click any video to jump to it instantly
  • Video thumbnails - High-quality preview images for each video
  • Per-video captions - Unique captions and subtitles for each video
  • Per-video chapters - Navigate video sections with chapter markers
  • Active video highlighting - Visual indicator for currently playing video
  • Full keyboard navigation - Tab, Enter, and Space for playlist control
  • Responsive design - Adapts to mobile, tablet, and desktop screens
  • Accessibility features - ARIA labels with duration announced to screen readers

Perfect For

  • TV Series - Binge-watch episodes
  • Video Courses - Sequential lessons
  • Conference Talks - Multiple presentations
  • Tutorial Series - Step-by-step guides
  • Film Collections - Short film festivals

Additional Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Shift + N - Next video
  • Shift + P - Previous video
